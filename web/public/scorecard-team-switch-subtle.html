Subtler "switch team" affordances

The shipped outline pill reads too loud. Here are quieter takes on the same bottom-of-list control — all still one thumb-tap, all naming Morpeth Jamiroquai (the team you'd switch to). Ordered roughly quietest → most present.

Quiet text
AGhost link
No pill, no border — just muted centred text "View Morpeth Jamiroquai ›". Reads as a quiet link, near-invisible until looked for.
BTiny crest + name
Small crest and "Switch to Morpeth Jamiroquai" in muted text, no container. The crest adds a dab of colour without a heavy pill.
DUnderlined link
A restrained underlined text link in a dimmed brand colour. Clearly tappable, no box.
Soft container
CSoft grey pill
Same shape as today but low-contrast: light grey fill, thin border, muted text, no shadow, no brand purple. Present but calm.
EFooter strip
A flat full-width footer: left shows "VIEWING · Brampton II Men", right is the tappable Morpeth ›. Feels built-in, not floating over rows.
Minimal icon
FCorner swap icon
Just the other team's crest as a small round button in the bottom-right thumb corner, with a tiny swap badge. The most out-of-the-way option.
